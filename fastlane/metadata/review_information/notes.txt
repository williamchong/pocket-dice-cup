Pocket Dice Cup is a dice cup: shake the iPhone to roll the dice, then lay it flat, screen up, or hold it still, and the total shows at the top. Tapping the table also rolls the dice, and the Settle button drops them at once. The dice button at the bottom picks which dice to roll.

There are no accounts, so no sign-in is needed. This version sells nothing. Haptics play on a real iPhone only. After a roll, the result is shown as a Live Activity in the Dynamic Island and on the Lock Screen.
