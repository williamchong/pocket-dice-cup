# Support

Pocket Dice Cup turns the phone into a dice cup: shake it, put it down
screen-up, read the dice.

## Using it

- **Roll:** shake the phone and put it down screen-up. With no motion sensor,
  as on a desktop, tap the cup or press the space bar.
- **Roll in the hand:** shake, then tap Settle, and the dice drop as if the
  phone lay flat.
- **Pick the dice:** tap the button at the bottom ("1d6") and use − and + for
  each kind, d4 to d20, up to 12 dice in all.
- **History:** the last 50 rolls, kept on the phone.
- **Style:** the finish of the dice.

## Common problems

- **The dice do not move when I shake (iPhone):** the app needs motion
  access. Tap the cup when it says "Tap to begin" and allow it. If it was
  refused, close the app completely and open it again to be asked again;
  until then, tap the cup to roll.
- **No sound of the dice (iPhone):** the clacks follow the silent switch, as
  game sounds do. Turn silent mode off to hear them.
