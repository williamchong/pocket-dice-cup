# Privacy Policy

Pocket Dice Cup, on iPhone, Android and the web, has no accounts and no
advertising. It sends usage statistics to Google Analytics, described below,
which you can switch off. The numbers you roll and your history stay on your
device.

## What stays on your device

The app keeps a few things in the phone's or browser's own storage, so they
are there next time:

- the dice you picked, such as "3d6";
- the finish of the dice, such as ivory, and the table they land on;
- the last 50 rolls, shown under History;
- whether Pro is owned, so its dice and tables show at once on launch;
- whether usage statistics are switched off.

None of this leaves the device. Clear, under History, removes the rolls;
deleting the app, or clearing the site's data in the browser, removes all
of it.

## Usage statistics

The app uses Google Analytics to show how it is used: through Google's script
on the web, and through Google's Firebase SDK in the phone apps. Google
processes this data for the app and receives:

- that a roll finished, with which dice, finish and table, such as "3d6" on
  the felt, but never the numbers rolled;
- in the phone apps, whether a purchase of Pro went through;
- the pages opened on the web, and the app being opened and how long it is used;
- the technical details these tools collect themselves, such as the device or
  browser type, the app version, the language, the approximate location Google
  derives from your IP address, and a random identifier for the browser or app
  installation, kept in a cookie on the web.

The app does not use advertising identifiers, does not use this data for
advertising, and does not link it to your name or account. It is kept for 2
months at the event level. See
[Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

To switch this off, open the about page (the question mark button) and turn
off **Share usage statistics** under Privacy. Your choice is kept on your
device. If you switch it off just after opening a page, the report of that
page opening may still be sent; nothing is sent after that.

## Motion sensor

The app reads the phone's motion sensor to tell when it is shaken and put
down. The readings are used on the spot to move the dice and are not stored
or sent anywhere. On iPhone the app asks for motion access first; refused,
the dice are rolled by tapping instead.

## Purchases

Pro, which unlocks more dice and tables, is bought in the iPhone app
through Apple's App Store and in the Android app through Google Play. The
store handles the payment and your account; the app only asks the store
whether Pro is owned and never sees your payment details, your Apple or
Google account, or the purchase's receipt. Apple's and Google's privacy
policies cover what they keep of the purchase. The web version sells
nothing.

## The web version

The web version is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub, like any web host, may
log the requests your browser makes for the page, such as its IP address;
see the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
The phone apps carry the page inside them rather than loading it from the
internet; they talk only to the App Store or Google Play, about Pro, and to
Google Analytics, as described above.

## Children

The app has no accounts and asks no one for personal details, children
included. The usage statistics above are collected the same way for everyone,
and can be switched off.
